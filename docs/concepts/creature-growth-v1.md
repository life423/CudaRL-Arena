## Identity to preserve

- Original fictional aquatic-to-terrestrial species, with a rounded expressive face and large recognizable eyes.
- Teal/jade upper body, pale cream underside and muted gold cheek markings.
- Translucent flowing fins and a dorsal ridge continuing into the long swimming tail.
- Consistent face, colors, markings and fin language throughout growth.
- Organic stylized game appearance with a credible weight-bearing body; preserve charm without making the adult excessively top-heavy.

## Three stages

1. **Aquatic baby:** horizontal swimming body and strong tapered tail; small side fins, no walking legs.
2. **First legs:** the same creature develops exactly two weight-bearing legs beneath a forming torso/pelvis. Articulated hips/knees and broad three-toed feet foreshadow bipedal balance. Retain side fins and tail.
3. **Upright adult:** compact vertical torso, pelvis over two sturdy legs, broad planted feet and the same recognizable face. The long tail extends behind as a counterbalance. Side appendages remain fins in this concept; articulated arms/hands are not a settled requirement.

Food-driven growth milestones are a proposed progression mechanic. The project's native neural network trained with PPO should discover joint control, balance and stepping. Designed growth stages are distinct from learned control and from biological evolution. Exact rig/joints, gravity/buoyancy/contact model, observation/action dimensions, policy transfer strategy and training curriculum still need design/testing. Visual approval does not authorize implementing the entire lifecycle at once.
