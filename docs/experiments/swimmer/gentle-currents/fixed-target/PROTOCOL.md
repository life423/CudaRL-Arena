- Fresh layout seeds `0xF17ED101`, `0xF17ED102`; 128 environments each. Fix the target to physical slot `environment % 3` until it is collected. Use the existing `deep_pond::observe` plus `primary_pond_food` target-promotion interface, retaining all 33 original inputs. The other two foods retain normal physical collection/respawn behavior; count incidental collections separately. This tests a fixed goal within the familiar three-food task, avoiding a missing-food observation-distribution change. It cannot prove the motor ignores the remaining food inputs.
- Paired normal starts and folded starts from the same body position/orientation and food layout. Normal uses the existing initializer. Folded replaces only joint angles with all 16 sign combinations at ±0.98 radians, cycling by environment. Velocities remain zero. This is recovery from independently defined starts, not injection of a known failure into training.
- Nine steady-current conditions: calm, and 0.5/1.0 cm/s each in upstream, downstream, cross-left and cross-right directions. Directions are relative to the initial horizontal body-to-target bearing and then remain fixed in world coordinates; they do not turn with the animal or guide it. Upstream water flows away from the target; downstream flows toward it. Food stays anchored until collection, matching the existing task. This is the existing imposed uniform-flow drag model, not CFD or a closed-pond circulation simulation.
- Include matched passive trials with all torques zero, using the same starting states, targets and currents. Distinguish water-relative motion, ground progress and passive advection. Passive drift is not learned propulsion.
- Primary endpoint: selected-target arrival by 60 seconds using the existing <0.24 model-unit 3D collection threshold. Secondary endpoint: arrival by 300 seconds, successful arrival times, minimum/remaining distance, worst continuous low-speed and no-progress spans, incidental foods, and mean ground/water-relative speed before arrival. Stop each trial at first target collection; no episode resets, rescue, target switching or post-success continuation. Do not count a frozen completed trial as stalled.
- Main batch: 2 seeds × 128 environments × 2 poses × 9 conditions × 2 controllers = 9,216 cases. Identical layouts are reused across paired conditions, not treated as independent layout samples. Primary summaries are descriptive; direction/layout repeats are not independent confidence-interval observations.
- Record distance-projected velocity separately from speed. Define low ground speed as <0.5 cm/s, and a no-progress window as 10 seconds without improving the best-so-far target distance by at least 0.05 model units (0.2 cm). These are diagnostic definitions, not training rewards, terminal rules or proof of a mechanical stall. Slow upstream motion can be physically correct.
- All cases continue to a five-minute horizon unless successful. Persist both 60-second and 300-second metrics, initial/final states and failure identities, so late recovery and stable failures remain distinguishable.
- Validate observation promotion, unchanged physical stepping, passive actions, target-only stopping and current bearings before interpreting results. Check motor weights and source bundle bytes after the run, and verify all protected training-save hashes. GPU results are authoritative for this diagnostic; do not silently mix CPU ranking results.

Decision rule: repeated fixed-target failures show a limitation in the existing motor/goal interface that the food selector alone cannot resolve while keeping that target. If fixed-target arrival is reliable in both poses but three-food selection fails, prioritize selector recovery. A failure does not by itself prove insufficient actuator strength; inspect goal conditioning, contact state and currents. Do not change the body, torque, drag or timestep to improve the score. Used layouts become recorded development evidence, not untouched holdouts for later model selection.
