# Held-out bank swims with frozen motor 88

The installed appended-16 chooser and the three-depth update-128 chooser ran the same eight approaches at each depth. Each swimmer began tangent to the bank with 15 mm certified whole-body clearance and had 180 simulated seconds. Direction is the signed tangent of the starting swim. Each policy chose its own actions.

The minimum gap is the native whole-body lower bound. A negative value triggered the clearance guard and does not establish physical impact. The stall column uses the longest consecutive native count at 20 ms per control. As in the original evaluator, stall counting begins after six seconds, so an early stop can have zero recorded stall. The following tables distinguish a completed swim with excess stall from a physical stop.
